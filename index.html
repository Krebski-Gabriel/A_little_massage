A letter for you

Hey, Jéssica…

I’ve been thinking about something, and I wanted to share it with you.

You know, sometimes I wonder how so many things can exist in this world — and somehow, among all of them, I got to meet you. I don’t think you realize just how much that means to me.

I mean, the stars are beautiful, and there’s something about the moon that makes everything feel a little more peaceful. But honestly, even the most beautiful things I can imagine don’t quite compare to how I feel when I’m with you.

And there’s something else I’ve been thinking about.

I don’t know what Heaven looks like. I don’t know what eternity feels like, either. But when I imagine spending my life with you, I think I get a little closer to understanding what those things might mean.

Because if I ever had to choose between seeing every beautiful thing this world has to offer, and having just one more moment with you… I think you already know what I’d choose.

I’d choose you, Jéssica. Every time.
